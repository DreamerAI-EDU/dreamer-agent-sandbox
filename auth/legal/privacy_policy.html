Part 1 — English Version

1. Introduction

DREAMER AI EDUCATION LIMITED ("Dreamer AI", "we", "us") operates the Dreamer AI learning platform at dreamer-aiedu.com and app.dreamer-aiedu.com. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect personal data in accordance with the Personal Data (Privacy) Ordinance (Cap. 486) of the Laws of Hong Kong ("PDPO").

Our learners are children. All accounts are created and held by parents or legal guardians, and students learn under their parent's account. By registering, the parent or legal guardian consents to this Policy on behalf of the student.

2. Information We Collect

From parents / guardians:

  • Full name
  • Email address
  • Telephone number

About students:

  • First name only (we never ask for or store a student's full name)
  • Age band (P1–P3 / P4–P6 / S1–S3)
  • Learning records: session logs, assessment records, portfolio items created during the course
  • Language preference (English / Cantonese / Putonghua)

Automatically collected: basic technical logs required to operate and secure the service. We do not run advertising trackers.

3. How We Use Information

We use the information above solely to:

  • Provide and operate the learning platform and tutoring services
  • Generate learning plans (reviewed and approved by Dreamer AI teachers before taking effect)
  • Produce progress reports and portfolios for parents
  • Protect student safety (see Section 6)
  • Communicate with parents about the service

We do not sell personal data. We do not use student data for advertising.

4. Research and Product Improvement

We may use learning data to improve our curriculum and platform. Such use is performed only on anonymised and aggregated data that cannot reasonably identify any student or parent. Identifiable learning records are never used for research or marketing purposes.

5. Third-Party Service Providers and Cross-Border Transfer

To operate the service, we engage the following categories of processors:

ProviderRoleLocation
OpenRouterAI language model processing of learning conversationsUnited States
HetznerServer hostingGermany
CloudflareDNS, security, and email routingUnited States

This means conversation content and account data are transferred outside Hong Kong. We select reputable providers and apply contractual and technical safeguards. By using the service, you acknowledge and consent to these transfers. This list of providers may change as our infrastructure evolves (for example, as we expand and adopt providers in other regions); any material change will be notified in advance under Section 11.

6. Student Safety (Welfare) Data

Our platform includes automated welfare detection designed to identify potential student distress or safety risks.

  • Welfare flags are sensitive data: access is restricted to authorised personnel only
  • Welfare records are retained for 90 days and then deleted
  • Where a high-risk event is detected, the designated Dreamer AI safeguarding staff member is notified directly so that the parent or guardian can be contacted promptly
  • Welfare data is used solely for protecting student safety and never for any other purpose

7. Data Retention

  • Learning records (session logs, assessments, portfolios): retained for 12 months after course completion, then deleted
  • Welfare flags: 90 days, then deleted
  • Account data: retained while the account is active; deleted upon account closure, subject to the learning-record schedule above
  • Backups are encrypted and expire on a rolling schedule consistent with the periods above

8. Children's Privacy and Parental Consent

  • Student profiles exist only under a parent/guardian account; students do not register independently
  • We collect only a first name and age band for each student — never a full name, school name, home address, or phone number
  • Students are taught, and the platform is designed, never to share personal details with the AI tutor
  • Parents may review, correct, or delete their child's data at any time (see Section 9)

9. Your Rights under the PDPO

As a parent or guardian, you have the right to:

  • Request access to the personal data we hold about you and your child
  • Request correction of inaccurate data
  • Request deletion of data (subject to the retention schedule in Section 7)
  • Withdraw consent to optional processing (e.g., media consent) at any time

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at info@dreamer-aiedu.com. We will respond within 30 days.

10. Security

We apply industry-standard safeguards, including HTTPS encryption in transit, access controls, firewall restrictions limiting public access to the minimum necessary, encrypted daily backups, and key-based server access.

11. Changes to This Policy

If we change this Policy, we will post the updated version on our website and notify parents by email before material changes take effect.

  • 年齡組別（P1–P3／P4–P6／S1–S3）

我們僅將上述資料用於：

我們絕不出售個人資料，亦不以學生資料作廣告用途。

6. 學生安全資料

本平台設有自動安全偵測，用以識別學生潛在之情緒困擾或安全風險。

  • 安全標記屬敏感資料：僅限獲授權人員查閱
  • 安全記錄保留 90 日，其後刪除
  • 偵測到高風險事件時，Dreamer AI 指定之學生保障負責人將直接獲通知，以便盡快聯絡家長或監護人
  • 安全資料僅用於保障學生安全，絕不作任何其他用途

7. 資料保留期

  • 學習記錄（課堂記錄、評估、作品集）：於課程完結後保留 12 個月，其後刪除
  • 安全標記：保留 90 日，其後刪除
  • 帳號資料：於帳號有效期間保留；帳號註銷後按上述學習記錄時間表刪除
  • 備份經加密處理，並按與上述一致之滾動時間表到期

  • 學生檔案僅存在於家長／監護人帳號之下；學生不得獨立註冊

  • 平台之設計及教學均以教導學生絕不向 AI 導師透露個人資料為本

  • 隨時撤回對可選擇處理（例如媒體同意）之同意

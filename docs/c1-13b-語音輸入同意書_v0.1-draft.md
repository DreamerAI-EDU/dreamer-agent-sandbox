## A. 繁體中文（zh-HK · 為準）

### 語音輸入同意書

DREAMER AI EDUCATION LIMITED

生效日期：＿＿＿＿＿＿＿＿（簽署日）

**1. 本文件之目的**

Dreamer AI 學習平台設有語音輸入功能（下稱「本功能」），讓學生按住麥克風按鈕講出問題，系統將語音轉為文字，顯示於學生之輸入框內，再由學生自行決定是否發送。本功能現於小一至小三（P1–P3）年級試行。本文件向家長或合法監護人說明本功能處理學生資料之方式，並徵求同意。

**2. 我們處理之資料**

- 學生按住麥克風按鈕期間錄取之一段語音（單段上限 60 秒）。本功能**並非全程收音**：不按掣不錄音，麥克風按鈕放開即停止。
- 該段語音轉出之文字（辨識結果）。

**3. 語音之處理及傳輸**

- 語音會即時傳送至第三方語音辨識服務供應商，以轉換為文字。目前使用之主要供應商為 **Microsoft Azure Speech（東亞區域）**；我們另配置一家備用供應商（Deepgram），僅在明確切換時方會使用，切換後適用相同規則。相關跨境傳輸安排載於私隱政策第 5 條。
- 傳送至供應商之資料**不包含**學生姓名、學號或任何可識別學生身分之資料。

**4. 原音即毀（錄音不會被保存）**

- 錄音不會寫入任何磁碟、不會存入資料庫，亦不會寫入系統日誌；該段錄音僅於處理該次請求期間存在於記憶體，請求完成後即時銷毀。
- 我們**不會**保存、回放或下載任何學生錄音。

**5. 我們不會做之用途**

- **不會**以錄音建立聲紋或作任何生物特徵辨識。
- **不會**將錄音或辨識文字用於訓練任何 AI 模型；供應商一方亦已設定不使用客戶資料作模型改進。
- **不會**將錄音或辨識文字用於廣告、宣傳或出售。

**6. 辨識文字之使用**

- 辨識結果僅顯示於學生本身之輸入框，**學生必須自行按下發送**，內容方會送出。此設計讓學生可先更正辨識錯誤。
- 若已簽署「AI 對話服務同意書」，發送後之文字即屬 AI 對話內容，按該同意書處理；語音系統本身不儲存辨識文字。

**7. 用量記錄**

- 系統僅記錄每日每名學生之語音使用分鐘數（用於成本控制及額度管理），**不含**錄音，亦**不含**辨識文字。
- 額度：單段上限 60 秒；每名學生每日 15 分鐘。額度用盡時麥克風按鈕不再顯示，學生可如常以文字輸入提問。

**8. 安全保障**

- 由語音轉出之文字與打字訊息採用同一套安全保障偵測，處理方式一致。
- 為保障學生安全，即使撤回本同意，如系統偵測到學生表達自我傷害或傷害他人之風險，我們仍會按私隱政策第 6 條採取保障措施，包括通知指定之學生保障負責人及提供求助支援熱線資訊。

**9. 閣下之選擇**

- 本功能為自願參與。若不同意本文件之處理安排，學生之介面**不會顯示麥克風按鈕**，其他功能不受影響，學生可如常以文字輸入。
- 閣下可**隨時撤回本同意**，電郵 info@dreamer-aiedu.com 即可；撤回後本功能即時停止運作，日後如要重新使用，須重新同意。
- 撤回本同意**不會**影響其他同意書之效力；其他同意書之撤回亦不會影響本同意。

## C. English (en)

### Voice Input Consent

DREAMER AI EDUCATION LIMITED

Effective Date: ____________________ (date of signature)

**1. What this form is about**

The Dreamer AI learning platform offers a voice input feature (the "Voice Input Feature") that lets a student hold the microphone button and speak a question; the speech is converted to text, shown in the student's own input box, and the student then decides whether to send it. The feature is currently being trialled with Primary 1 to Primary 3 (P1–P3) pupils. This form explains to the parent or legal guardian how the feature processes student data and seeks your consent.

**2. What we process**

- One clip of speech captured while the student holds the microphone button (maximum 60 seconds per clip). The microphone is **not always on**: nothing is recorded until the button is held, and recording stops when it is released.
- The text produced from that clip (the recognition result).

**3. How the speech is processed and transferred**

- The clip is transmitted in real time to a third-party speech recognition provider for conversion into text. Our primary provider is **Microsoft Azure Speech (East Asia region)**. A reserve provider (Deepgram) is configured but will only be used if we expressly switch to it, under the same rules. The related cross-border transfer arrangements are set out in Section 5 of the Privacy Policy.
- The data sent to the provider contains **no** student name, student ID, or any other identifier of the student.

**4. The audio is destroyed immediately**

- The clip is never written to any disk, never stored in the database, and never written to system logs. It exists in memory only for the duration of that one request and is destroyed as soon as the request completes.
- We do **not** keep, replay, or download any student recording.

**5. What we never do with it**

- We do **not** create voiceprints or perform any biometric identification.
- We do **not** use the audio or the recognised text to train any AI model; the provider side is likewise configured not to use customer data for model improvement.
- We do **not** use the audio or the recognised text for advertising, promotion, or sale.

**6. How the recognised text is used**

- The recognition result appears only in the student's own input box, and **the student must press send themselves** for it to be submitted. This is deliberate: it lets the child correct a mis-heard word.
- If the AI Chat Service Consent has been signed, the text becomes AI chat content once sent and is handled under that consent. The voice system itself does not store the recognised text.

**7. Usage records**

- We record only the number of voice minutes used per student per day (for cost control and quota management). The record contains **no** audio and **no** recognised text.
- Limits: maximum 60 seconds per clip; 15 minutes per student per day. When the quota is exhausted the microphone button is no longer shown, and the student can ask questions by typing as usual.

**8. Safety**

- Text produced from speech goes through the same safety detection as typed messages, treated identically.
- For the protection of student safety, even after withdrawal of this consent, if the system detects that a student expresses a risk of self-harm or harm to others, we will still take protective measures in accordance with Section 6 of the Privacy Policy, including notifying the designated student safeguarding staff member and providing helpline support information.

**9. Your choice**

- Participation is voluntary. If you do not consent to the processing described in this form, **no microphone button will be shown** in your child's interface; nothing else is affected, and the child can type as usual.
- You may **withdraw this consent at any time** by emailing info@dreamer-aiedu.com. Upon withdrawal the Voice Input Feature stops immediately; using it again later requires fresh consent.
- Withdrawing this consent does **not** affect the validity of any other consent form, and withdrawing another form does not affect this one.

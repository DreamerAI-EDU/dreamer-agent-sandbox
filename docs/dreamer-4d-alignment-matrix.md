## 1. Purpose

This document provides the internal alignment between Dreamer AI Education's proprietary competency framework and the International Baccalaureate (IB) Approaches to Learning (ATL) skills and Learner Profile.

The Dreamer AI Competency Framework (Dream, Discover, Design, Deliver) is the **brand-facing layer** for parents and students. The IB ATL skills and Learner Profile serve as the **academic validity layer** — the language used when speaking to schools, applying for accelerator programmes, and writing partnership proposals.

## 2. Framework Overview

### 2.1 IB Approaches to Learning (ATL) Skills

Five interrelated skill clusters spanning all IB programmes (PYP, MYP, DP, CP):

| ATL Skill Cluster | Sub-Skills Relevant to AI Literacy |
|---|---|
| **Thinking Skills** | Critical thinking (evaluating AI outputs, fact-checking), creative thinking (prompt iteration, ideation), transfer (applying AI skills across contexts) |
| **Research Skills** | Information literacy (evaluating AI-generated information), media literacy (understanding AI content creation), ethical use of information (crediting AI tools, recognising bias) |
| **Communication Skills** | Exchanging information (bilingual expression), literacy (narrative creation), ICT literacy (using AI tools effectively) |
| **Self-Management Skills** | Organisation (portfolio curation, project planning), reflection (learning journal, ethical self-assessment), perseverance (iterating on AI outputs) |
| **Social Skills** | Collaboration (working with AI as a partner), ethical responsibility (understanding AI's societal impact) |

### 2.2 IB Learner Profile

Ten attributes familiar to HK parents and teachers:

| Attribute | AI Literacy Expression |
|---|---|
| Inquirers | Curiosity, prompt iteration, exploration |
| Thinkers | Critical evaluation of AI outputs, ethical reasoning |
| Communicators | Bilingual expression, presentation skills |
| Principled | Ethical AI use, crediting tools, academic honesty |
| Reflective | Learning journal, bias awareness, self-assessment |
| Risk-takers | Experimenting with new AI tools, creative risk-taking |
| Knowledgeable | Understanding AI concepts, tools, and limitations |
| Caring | Considering AI's societal and ethical implications |
| Open-minded | Comparing AI and human perspectives |
| Balanced | Healthy screen habits, balanced use of AI tools |

### 2.3 Dreamer AI 4D Competency Framework

Brand-facing, proprietary framework for parent and student communication. Parents and students never hear "ATL" or "Learner Profile" — they hear this:

| Phase | Label (EN) | Label (ZH) | Focus |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Dream** | Imagine | 想像 | Creative thinking, curiosity, storytelling, ideation |
| **Discover** | Think | 探究 | AI literacy, critical thinking, prompt engineering, ethics |
| **Design** | Create | 創造 | Video, games, writing, visual communication — tangible outputs |
| **Deliver** | Share | 分享 | Presentation, bilingual communication, reflection, confidence |

## 6. IB-Aligned Assessment Rubric (0-3 Scale)

| ATL Skill → 4D | 0 — Limited | 1 — Developing | 2 — Proficient | 3 — Excellent |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Thinking (Creative) → Dream | Uses first AI prompt only; no refinement | Iterates 1-2 times with guidance | Iterates 3+ times independently; explores angles | Compares multiple outputs; combines elements into original work |
| Research (Info literacy) → Discover | Accepts all AI outputs without question | Verifies one fact with prompting | Independently verifies; identifies inaccuracy | Cross-references multiple sources; documents verification |
| Communication (Exchanging) → Deliver | One language only; unclear delivery | Bilingual but incomplete; lacks confidence | Complete bilingual; clear pacing and eye contact | Polished bilingual; confident, engaging, responsive |
| Self-Management (Reflection) → Deliver | No reflection or single sentence | Describes what was done, no analysis | Identifies specific learning and challenges | Analyses growth over time; connects to real-world AI use |

## 10. Strategic Note: Why IB over P21 4C

This document replaces the previous P21 4C alignment. Rationale:

1. **HK Market Reality:** 40+ schools in Hong Kong follow IB programmes — Dreamer AI's primary partnership targets
2. **Assessment Rigour:** IB's criterion-based rubrics (0-3 / 0-8 with detailed descriptors) are more credible to professional judges and school partners than 4C's single-question-per-pillar approach
3. **Natural Fit with AI Literacy:** ATL's Thinking / Research / Communication / Self-Management / Social map directly to AI literacy competencies; 4C misses Self-Management (critical for AI literacy)
4. **Parent Communication:** IB Learner Profile attributes are familiar to HK international school parents
5. **Defensibility:** "IB-aligned" is a credible positioning statement; "4C Framework" carries less weight in HK
6. **Long-Term Value:** ATL provides a developmental progression that scales from beginner to advanced; 4C is flat

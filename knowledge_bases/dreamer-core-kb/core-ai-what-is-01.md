# What Is AI? From Rules to Learning

## Dream Phase: Curiosity Spark

Welcome to the world of artificial intelligence. In this Dream phase topic, students are invited to wonder: what does it mean for a machine to be "smart"?

### Hook: Can a Computer Think?

Let's start with a simple question. When you tell a calculator "2 + 2", it gives you "4" instantly. Is that thinking? When you ask a video game enemy to chase you, it follows. Is that intelligence?

The answer depends on what we mean by "thinking" — and that's exactly what the Dream phase is for: asking big questions before we start building.

### Two Kinds of Smart

| Type | How It Works | Example |
|------|-------------|---------|
| Rule-Based | The programmer writes every rule in advance | A chess engine evaluating millions of positions |
| Learning-Based | The program learns patterns from examples | A photo app recognizing your cat after seeing many cat pictures |

### Activity: Rule Learner vs. Pattern Learner

Students work in pairs. One group writes explicit rules for sorting shapes by color and size. Another group is given 20 labeled examples and asked to sort new shapes. Discussion follows: which approach was faster? Which handled surprises better?

### Reflection Questions

1. What surprised you about how a "learning" machine works differently from a "rule" machine?
2. Can you think of something in your daily life that might use learning-based AI?
3. If a machine can learn, does that mean it understands?

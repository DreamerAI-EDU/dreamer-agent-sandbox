# 🌟 Week 1: This is Me! (Dream Phase)

**Focus: Introduction to AI, Self-Expression, and Digital Citizenship**

**Primary Skills Focus: Creative Thinking**

**AI Tools: DreamerAI (for text), Canva (for images)**


## 🕐 5-Minute Citizenship Minute: "Who is Behind the Screen?"

**Topic: Privacy and Personal Information**

**Context: In Hong Kong, we use apps like WhatsApp and Instagram every day. But when we talk to AI, who is listening?**

**Discussion Script:**

Ask: "Raise your hand if you have ever talked to Siri, Alexa, or any LLM model e.g. Gemini, Poe,Grok? . Did you tell it a secret?"

Explain: "AI is like a very smart parrot. It listens to everything you say to learn. But unlike a best friend, AI doesn't have a 'secret button.' It can remember what you say and use it to answer other people."

**The Rule: "Never share your full name, your home address, your school name, or your phone number with AI. Treat AI like a stranger on the MTR—be polite, but don't give them your private info."**


## 📝 4-Hour Self-Learning Mission Sheet

**Mission Goal: Create a "Super Student Profile" that introduces you to the class using AI.**


### Task 1: The AI Interview (English Written)

**Tool: DreamerAI**

**Time: 60 minutes**

**Instructions:**

1. Open DreamerAI.

2. Copy and paste this prompt:

"Hello! I am a Primary 6 student in Hong Kong. I want you to act as a friendly reporter. Please ask me 5 fun questions about my hobbies, my favorite food, and what I want to be when I grow up. Wait for my answers before asking the next question."

3. Answer the questions one by one.

4. Reflection Question: Did the AI understand my answers? Did it ask follow-up questions?


### Task 2: My Avatar (Creative Design)

**Tool: Canva (Magic Media)**

**Time: 90 minutes**

**Instructions:**

1. Log in to Canva. Create a new design (Custom Size: 800x800 px).

2. Click "Apps" and select "Magic Media."

3. Copy and paste this prompt:

"A cartoon avatar of a 12-year-old student in Hong Kong school uniform, [insert hair color and style], [insert skin tone], holding a [insert favorite hobby item], bright and colorful style, flat vector illustration."

4. Generate the image until you like it. Add your name to the bottom.

5. Reflection Question: Does this avatar really look like me? What is missing?


### Task 3: My Story in Chinese (Chinese Written)

**Tool: DreamerAI (set language to Chinese if possible, or translate)**

**Time: 60 minutes**

**Instructions:**

1. Think about your avatar. What is their superpower?

2. Copy and paste this prompt:

"請幫我寫一段關於我的卡通頭像的自我介紹。我的名字是 [你的名字]，我的超能力是 [你的超能力，例如：跑得快、很會畫畫]。請用小學生的語氣，大約 50 字。"

3. Copy the text and paste it onto your Canva image.

4. Reflection Question: Is the Chinese natural? Did I need to fix any words?


### Task 4: Introduce Yourself! (English Oral)

**Tool: Video Recorder (Phone/Computer) or Voice Recorder**

**Time: 30 minutes**

**Instructions:**

1. Look at your Canva poster.

2. Record a 1-minute video introducing yourself.

Say: "Hi, I'm [Name]. My avatar shows me holding [Item] because I love [Hobby]. In the future, I want to be a [Job]."

3. Watch your video. Check your volume and speed.

4. Reflection Question: Did I speak clearly? Can I improve my pronunciation?


## 🏫 F2F Coaching Session Structure (60 Minutes)

**Goal: Review work, practice speaking, and build confidence.**

| Time | Activity | Details |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 5 min | Citizenship Check | Ask: "What personal info did we keep private last week?" |
| 10 min | Mission Review | Students show their Canva posters. Teacher picks 2-3 interesting ones to share. |
| 20 min | Demo: Prompt Engineering | Show how changing one word in a prompt (e.g., "sad" to "happy") changes the image. |
| 15 min | Guided Practice | Students help each other improve one prompt for their avatar. |
| 10 min | Oral Practice | Pair students up. They practice their 1-minute intro with a partner. |
| 10 min | Confidence Check | "Thumbs up, middle, or down: How confident do you feel about using AI?" |


## 📊 Rubric: Creative Thinking (Level 0-3)

| Level | Rating | Criteria |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 3 | Exemplary | I used AI to create a unique avatar and story. I combined ideas in a new way. My oral presentation was clear and confident. |
| 2 | Achieved | I created an avatar and wrote a story with AI's help. I completed my oral presentation. |
| 1 | Developing | I made an avatar, but it doesn't look like me. I needed a lot of help with the story. My presentation was very quiet. |
| 0 | Not Yet | I did not complete the mission or I did not use the AI tools. |

# 🎬 Week 3: Movie Creator (Design & Deliver Phase)

**Focus: Copyright Awareness, Scriptwriting, and Media Production**

**Primary Skills Focus: Communication Skills & Research Skills (Media Literacy)**

**AI Tools: DreamerAI (for scriptwriting), Canva (for video creation)**


## 🕐 5-Minute Citizenship Minute: "Who Owns the Movie?"

**Topic: Copyright and Responsible AI Content**

**Context: We love watching movies and videos. But who made them? Can we just copy them?**

**Discussion Script:**

Ask: "If you drew a beautiful picture, how would you feel if someone took it, put their name on it, and sold it?"

Explain: "Copyright means 'the right to copy.' When someone creates something—a song, a story, a video—they own it. Using AI to copy someone else's work is like stealing their idea."

**The Golden Rule: "Use AI to create new things, not to copy existing things. Always credit your sources. Be an original creator, not a copycat."**


## 📝 4-Hour Self-Learning Mission Sheet

**Mission Goal: Create a 10 second short video about a topic you care about (e.g., saving the ocean, why homework matters, your favorite hobby) using AI to help write the script.**


### Task 1: The Scriptwriter's Challenge (English Written)

**Tool: DreamerAI**

**Time: 90 minutes**

**Instructions:**

1. Choose a topic for your video.

2. Step A (First Draft): Ask DreamerAI to write a script for your topic.

3. Step B (The Critique): Read the script. Is it too long? Is it boring? Does it sound like a robot wrote it?

4. Step C (The Rewrite): Use this prompt to improve it:

"This is a script for a 10-second video about [topic]. The current script is [paste script]. Please rewrite it to be more exciting,  attention."

use simple words for P6 students, and include a hook (an interesting opening sentence) to grab

5. Reflection Question: How did you change the script? Why is your version better than the AI's first version?


### Task 2: The Video Production (Creative Design)

**Tool: Canva Video**

**Time: 90 minutes**

**Instructions:**

1. Create a new video project.

2. Important Rule: You must use at least 3 different types of media:

Text (titles/captions)

Images (from Canva's free library or AI-generated)

Your own voice recording

3. Constraint: If using AI images, you must add your own text overlays or effects to show your creativity. Don't just paste AI images directly.

**💡 Hints for Task 2:**

Need background music? Search for "royalty-free" music to avoid copyright issues.

Images look fake? Use Canva's "Edit Image" to adjust brightness or add filters.

Script too long? Cut it down! 10 seconds = about 20 words maximum.

Transition stuck? Use simple fade effects between scenes.

4. Reflection Question: What part of the video was hardest to make? Did you use any AI tools to help?


### Task 3: Fact-Check Report (Chinese Written)

**Tool: DreamerAI & Web Search**

**Time: 60 minutes**

**Instructions:**

1. Your video includes at least one fact (e.g., "80% of the ocean is unexplored" or "who is the first MVP in NBA league").

2. Use Google or another search engine to verify this fact.

3. Write a short report in Chinese explaining your findings.


## 📝 Example: 事實查核報告

Fact in video: "香港每年有超過 100 萬噸廢物被送往堆填區。"

Fact-Check Result:

事實查核報告

聲稱： 香港每年有超過 100 萬噸廢物被送往堆填區。

查核結果： 根據環保署 2023 年的數據，香港每日產生約 11,000 公噸廢物，其中約 30% 被送往堆填區。換算成每年，大約有 400 萬噸廢物送往堆填區。

結論： 我的聲稱是錯誤的。實際數字比聲稱的更高。我需要修改視頻中的數據。

4. Reflection Question: Was the fact in your video correct? If not, how will you fix it?


### Task 4: The Director's Commentary (English Oral)

**Tool: Phone/ Audio Recorder**

**Time: 30 minutes**

**Instructions:**

1. Record a 1-minute "Director's Commentary" about your video.

2. Structure:

0:00-0:20: What inspired your topic?

0:20-0:40: What was the hardest part of making this video?

0:40-1:00: What are you most proud of?

3. Reflection Question: Did I speak clearly about my creative process?


## 🏫 F2F Coaching Session Structure (60 Minutes)

**Goal: Focus on media literacy—understanding how to create and evaluate content responsibly in a 1-on-1 setting.**

| Time | Activity | Details |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 5 min | Citizenship Check | "Did you use any images or music that might belong to someone else? How did you check?" |
| 10 min | Mission Review | Watch the student's video together. Ask: "What message are you trying to send?" |
| 20 min | Demo: Copyright Check | Teacher shows how to find "royalty-free" images and music. Discuss what "fair use" means. |
| 15 min | Guided Practice | Fact-Check Together: Pick one fact from the student's video and search for it together. Discuss how to tell if a source is reliable. |
| 10 min | Oral Prep | Commentary Outline: Help student outline their 3 talking points for the Director's Commentary. |
| 10 min | Confidence Check | "On a scale of 1-5, how confident do you feel about creating original media content?" |

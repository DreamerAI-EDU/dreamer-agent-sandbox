# 🌟 Week 6: My Big Idea — Ethical AI in the Classroom (Dream & Discover Phase)

**Focus: AI for Social Good — Creating a responsible AI-use policy**

**Primary Skills Focus: Research Skills & Thinking Skills**

**AI Tools: Dreamer AI (for policy drafting), Canva / Google Docs (for final poster/policy)**


## 🕐 5-Minute Citizenship Minute: "AI for Social Good — Can AI Make School Better?"

**Topic: AI for Education and Equity**

**Context: AI isn't just fun games — it can help teachers, help students with disabilities, and make learning fairer. But only if we use it with rules.**

**Discussion Script:**

Ask: "If you could give AI one job in your classroom, what would it be? (Grade papers? Help with math? Translate for new students?)"

Explain: "AI can be a great tool for social good — but only if the rules are fair. A good AI policy protects privacy, says when AI helps and when it doesn't, and makes sure everyone is included."

**Challenge: "This week, YOU are the rule-maker. Build a classroom AI-use policy that is fair, clear, and useful."**


## 📝 4-Hour Self-Learning Mission Sheet

**Mission Goal: Use Dreamer AI to generate draft policy ideas, lead a class discussion, create the final policy, and evaluate it.**


### Task 1 — Generate with AI (English, Bilingual Elements)

**Tool: Dreamer AI + your image steps**

**Time: 60 minutes**

**Instructions (follow the 4 steps):**

STEP 1: Use Dreamer AI to create your ideas. Copy and paste:

"Act as a P6 student in Hong Kong. I want to create a classroom AI-use policy. Please ask me: What are the values and norms you want in our classroom? How can you make space for discussion when AI is used? How can you make sure everyone can use AI fairly?"

STEP 2: Facilitate your own class discussion (or 1-on-1 with your teacher).

"Use your notes and your student's responses to discuss: When is AI okay to use? When is it not okay? How do we keep our work original?"

STEP 3: Create an appropriate use policy. Use Dreamer AI again:

"Please help me turn my discussion into a simple, 4-point classroom AI-use policy in English and Chinese. Include: 1) When AI is allowed, 2) When it is not allowed, 3) How to check if AI is correct, 4) How to protect privacy."

STEP 4: Evaluate the suggestions. Write 3 answers in your notebook:

Did Dreamer AI accurately reflect the values we discussed?

Are there any changes or updates needed?

How can you make the final policy better?

**Reflection Question: Did the AI suggestions match what our class really cares about? What did I have to change?**


### Task 2 — My Local Problem (Chinese Written + English Oral Prep)

**Tool: Dreamer AI / Notes**

**Time: 60 minutes**

**Instructions:**

1. Pick a real local Hong Kong problem for your class: education, environment, or accessibility.

2. Write 1 paragraph in Chinese describing it:

"香港有一個關於 [問題] 的問題。現在，我想用 AI 幫助 [解決方法]。"

3. Then ask Dreamer AI:

"I want to use AI for this problem: [your problem]. Give me 3 simple AI solutions that are fair for all students."

4. Reflection: Which solution is most realistic? Why?


### Task 3 — The Policy Poster (Creative Design)

**Tool: Canva**

**Time: 90 minutes**

**Instructions:**

1. Design a poster (A4 / 1080px) titled: "Our Classroom AI Rules."

2. Include:

Your 4-point policy in English

Your 4-point policy in Chinese

One visual / icon representing "fair use"

3. Constraint: You must generate one image with Canva Magic Media, but draw/edit the rest yourself to show your own design.

4. Prompt for AI image:

"A poster design showing students using AI together fairly, classroom setting, colorful flat illustration, clean and simple style."

5. Reflection: Does this poster make the rules clear to someone who didn't attend the discussion?


### Task 4 — The Pitch & Reflection (English Oral)

**Tool: Video / Voice recorder**

**Time: 30 minutes**

**Instructions:**

1. Record a 2-minute video:

0:00-0:45: Show your poster. Explain your 4 rules and why they matter.

0:45-1:30: Explain your local Hong Kong problem and which AI solution you'd try.

1:30-2:00: What's the strongest part of your policy? What's one thing you would improve?

2. Reflection Question: Did I sound confident when I explained the rules?


## 🏫 F2F Coaching Session Structure (60 Minutes)

**Goal: Focus on responsible innovation and stakeholder impact in a 1-on-1 setting.**

| Time | Activity | Details |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 5 min | Citizenship Check | "What value did your policy protect — privacy, fairness, or original work? Why does that matter?" |
| 10 min | Mission Review | Student presents her poster. Ask: "Would a new student understand these rules from looking at this?" |
| 20 min | Demo: Policy Refinement | Teacher picks one of her 4 rules. Together, ask Dreamer AI: "How can this rule be clearer? What is missing?" Live-edit the policy based on feedback. |
| 15 min | Guided Practice | Stakeholder Impact: Ask "How would this policy affect a student with dyslexia? How would it affect a student with no computer at home?" Discuss fairness. |
| 10 min | Oral Prep | Pitch Outline: Student practices her 3-part video — poster explanation, problem, reflection. |
| 10 min | Confidence Check | "On a scale of 1-5, how confident are you that your AI rules are fair for everyone?" |


## 📊 Rubric: Research Skills & Thinking Skills (Level 0-3)

| Level | Research Skills | Thinking Skills |

# 🧠 Week 7: Thinking Traps & Alternative Thoughts (Design & Deliver Phase)

**Focus: Critical Thinking — Identifying cognitive distortions and replacing them**

**Primary Skills Focus: Thinking Skills (Critical) & Self-Management**

**AI Tools: DreamerAI (for reflection prompt), Canva  (Task 1)**


## 🕐 5-Minute Citizenship Minute: "Are Your Thoughts Trapping You?"

**Topic: Thinking Traps and Responsible Mindset**

**Context: AI can help us learn, but if we say "I'm bad at this" or "AI is too hard," we stop growing. These are "thinking traps" — patterns in our minds that limit us.**

**Discussion Script:**

Ask: "Have you ever thought 'I'm just not smart enough for AI' when something went wrong? Did that thought help you, or stop you?"

Explain: "A 'thinking trap' is like a fake rule your brain makes up. The comic you will design shows a pig who thinks everything is the end of the world, then changes his thought to a better one. You can do that too."

**The Rule: "When you catch a thinking trap, don't just fight it — replace it with a fair, real thought. That's how you grow."**


## 📝 4-Hour Self-Learning Mission Sheet

**Mission Goal: Design a 4-panel comic that shows YOUR thinking trap + an alternative thought, using the reference image as a model.**


### Task 1 — The Thinking Trap Comic (Creative Design)

**Tool: Canva / Comic Creator / Paper + Scanner**

**Time: 90 minutes**

**Instructions (follow the 4-panel reference image):**

Your comic must have exactly 4 panels like the reference comic:

| Panel | Content | Example from reference |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Panel 1 — The Trigger | What happened? | Pig sees something he doesn't like |
| Panel 2 — The Trap Thought | Your bad thought / "thinking trap" | "I am terrible at AI. I will never understand this." |
| Panel 3 — The Alternative | A fair, realistic replacement thought | "I'm still learning. It's okay to make mistakes." |
| Panel 4 — The New Feeling / Action | How you feel or what you do now | You try again with more patience |

**Steps:**

1. Choose your trap: Pick one real thought you have about AI or school (e.g., "I'm bad at technology," "Everyone else is faster," "If I fail once, I'm a failure").

2. Draw / design your 4-panel comic. Use simple colors. You can draw the characters stick-style or use Canva elements.

3. Add text bubbles in English and Chinese for Panels 2 and 3 (the trap and the alternative).

4. Reference image reminder: The pig comic uses simple illustrations with text above/inside. Make your style clear and easy to read — no tiny text.

**💡 Hints for Task 1:**

Stuck on the trap? Ask yourself: "When I make a mistake with AI, what do I say to myself?" That's the trap.

Need the Chinese side? Use simple phrases: "我永遠學不會" → "我還在學習，這很正常"

Don't have drawing skills? Use Canva shapes, speech bubbles, and stick-figure tools. The message is what matters.

**Reflection: Which panel was hardest to draw — the trap or the alternative?**


### Task 2 — The Trap Log (English Written)

**Tool: Notebook / DreamerAI (for prompt help if needed)**

**Time: 60 minutes**

**Instructions:**

1. Write a short log of a real moment this week when you caught yourself in a thinking trap.

2. Use this format:

Date: ___

What happened: ___

My thinking trap: ___ (e.g., "I'll never get this")

Alternative thought: ___ (e.g., "I need more practice, not more pressure")

How I felt after: ___

3. Prompt Template (optional, for help):

"I caught myself thinking [trap]. Help me write a 3-sentence alternative that is kind but true."

**Reflection Question: Did replacing the thought actually change how you felt?**


### Task 3 — Prototype the Alternative (Design Phase)

**Tool: Scratch / Canva**

**Time: 90 minutes**

**Instructions:**

1. Take your alternative thought from Task 1 and turn it into a small action or design.

2. Examples:

Design a "Positive Thought Card" in Canva with your comic image + the alternative phrase.

Build a simple Scratch animation showing the trap → alternative → success.

Create a "Thought Checker" checklist: 3 questions to ask yourself when you feel stuck.

3. Constraint: You must include both English and Chinese text.

**Reflection: How did designing the alternative help you understand the trap better?**


### Task 4 — The Confident Pitch (English Oral)

**Tool: Video / Voice recorder**

**Time: 30 minutes**

**Instructions:**

1. Record a 2-minute presentation:

0:00-0:45: Show your comic. Explain the trap and the alternative in your own words.

0:45-1:30: Show your prototype / card / design. Why does this help someone else?

1:30-2:00: What's one trap you watch for now? What's one way you'll keep using alternatives?

2. Reflection: Did I sound proud of growing from the trap?


## 🏫 F2F Coaching Session Structure (60 Minutes)

**Goal: Focus on risk-taking and inquiry — helping her identify her own traps, not just copy the comic.**

| Time | Activity | Details |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 5 min | Citizenship Check | "What's one thinking trap you noticed in yourself this week — not just in the comic?" |
| 10 min | Mission Review | Student shows her 4-panel comic (Task 1). Ask: "Which panel is your real feeling? How does the alternative change the story?" |
| 20 min | Demo: Thinking Trap Mapping | Teacher draws a quick 4-box grid together with the student for one of her own real school/AI moments. Model identifying the trigger, the trap, the alternative, and the result. |
| 15 min | Guided Practice | Risk-Taking: Help her try one "alternative thought" in real time — e.g., if she says "I'm bad at this," coach her to say "I'm new at this" and then try one small step. |
| 10 min | Oral Prep | Pitch Outline: Student practices her 2-minute presentation — comic, prototype, growth reflection. |
| 10 min | Confidence Check | "On a scale of 1-5, how confident are you at catching your own thinking traps?" |


## 📊 Rubric: Thinking Skills (Critical) & Self-Management (Level 0-3)

| Level | Thinking Skills | Self-Management |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 3 | Exemplary | I clearly identified my trap, explained why it's unfair, and designed a realistic alternative. My comic uses the 4-panel format correctly. I caught a real trap in my life, wrote an honest log, and used an alternative to change how I felt or acted. |
| 2 | Achieved | I made a complete 4-panel comic with trap and alternative. I explained my reasoning. I wrote my log and tried to use an alternative at least once. |
| 1 | Developing | My comic is simple or mostly copied from the reference image without my own trap/alternative. I wrote a log but didn't really try to change the thought in real life. |
| 0 | Not Yet | I did not make a comic or identify a real trap. I didn't try to observe or change my thinking. |

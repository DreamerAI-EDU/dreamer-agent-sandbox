# 🌟 Week 8: Showcase & Reflect — "Don't Take It at Face Value" (Ethics Summary & Final Portfolio)

**Focus: Responsible AI Future + Final Portfolio**

**Primary Skills Focus: All — Reflective, Principled, Balanced**

**AI Tools: DreamerAI (for reflection prompts), Canva / Document (portfolio)**


## 🕐 5-Minute Citizenship Minute: "Don't Take It at Face Value"

**Topic: Ethics Summary — Responsible AI Future**

**Discussion Script:**

Ask: "If a news reporter says 'AI is dangerous,' should you believe it immediately? What's the difference between 'close' (feeling right) and 'objective' (proven)?"

Explain: "AI can make things look very real — photos, news, voices. The comic reminds us: don't take a headline just because it feels close or scary. Check what's objective — the real source, the real fact, the real proof."

**Challenge: Today you become the reporter. You don't just report the news — you report the truth behind it.**


## 📝 4-Hour Self-Learning Mission Sheet

**Mission Goal: Build your final portfolio + record a 3-min bilingual showcase. Include a news report and an online reporter role-play.**


### Task 1 — The Digital Portfolio (Design Phase)

**Tool: Canva / Google Docs / Folder**

**Time: 90 minutes**

**Instructions:**

1. Gather all 8 weeks of work:

Week 1: Avatar + self-intro

Week 2: PE-day solution

Week 3: Short video

Week 4: Game

Week 5: Detective Notes / Fact-check

Week 6: AI policy poster

Week 7: Thinking Traps comic

2. Create one final "Digital Portfolio" page or folder with:

Title page with your name and a title

One image or screenshot from each week

A short caption for each (2 sentences in English, 1 in Chinese)

3. Reflection (English + Chinese):

"This week I learned… / 这星期我学会了…"

"My biggest growth was… / 我最大的成长是…"


### Task 2 — The News Report (English Written — Become a Reporter)

**Tool: Notes / Word / DreamerAI (only for checking, not writing)**

**Time: 60 minutes**

**Instructions:**

1. Write a real news report about one topic from your journey — e.g., "How AI Helps P6 Students Learn" or "The Truth Behind AI Images in Class."

2. Use the comic's rule: Don't take it at face value.

3. Your report must include:

Headline: Clear and factual

Lead paragraph: What happened / what is the issue?

Evidence: At least one fact you verified yourself (not just AI)

Source: Where did you check it?

Conclusion: What's the objective truth?

Prompt Template (for structure, not content):

"Help me structure a 150-word news report about [topic]. Give me a headline, lead, evidence section, and source section — but I will write the content myself."

**Reflection: Why did you choose this topic? How did checking sources make it stronger?**


### Task 3 — The Online Reporter (Oral Practice — Role-Play)

**Tool: Video / Voice / Phone camera**

**Time: 30 minutes**

**Instructions (Pretend you are an online news reporter):**

1. Record yourself as a reporter on video (1–2 minutes):

Greeting: "Welcome to [Your Name] News. Today we're reporting on [topic from Task 2]."

Body: Present your evidence like a reporter — not too fast, not too slow. Use a clear voice.

The Check: End with: "We didn't take this at face value. We checked [source]. The objective truth is [your conclusion]."

2. Bilingual option: You can speak mostly English with Chinese subtitles or captions, OR do 1 minute English / 30 seconds Chinese explanation.

3. Reflection: Did you sound like a reporter? Did you stick to the facts?


### Task 4 — The Final Reflection — DreamerAI Learner Profile (Chinese + English)

**Tool: Document**

**Time: 30 minutes**

**Instructions:**

1. Write two short reflections:

In English: "How have I grown as a Reflective, Principled, and Balanced learner during these 8 weeks?"

In Chinese: "在這8週的AI課程中，我學會了什麼？我最骄傲的是哪一個任務？"

2. Choose one Learner Profile attribute (e.g., Reflective, Principled, Open-Minded, Balanced) and explain:

Before: How were you before this course?

After: How are you now?

One example from your portfolio that proves it.


## 🏫 F2F Coaching Session Structure (60 Minutes)

**Goal: Celebration, final portfolio review, and confidence check — 1-on-1.**

| Time | Activity | Details |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 5 min | Citizenship Check | "What is one 'Don't Take It at Face Value' rule you'll keep using for the rest of your school life?" |
| 10 min | Mission Review | Student presents her portfolio. Teacher asks: "Which week's deliverable shows your best growth? Why?" |
| 20 min | Demo / Guided Practice | The Reporter Test: Watch her news report video together (Task 3). Give feedback on: clarity, evidence, and whether she stuck to the objective truth (not just feeling/close). |
| 15 min | Guided Practice: Reflection Deep-Dive | Together, she writes/finalizes her Learner Profile reflection (Task 4). Ask "why" 3 times to make it specific. |
| 10 min | Oral Prep / Final Pitch | She records her 3-minute final showcase presentation — or practices the final version. Ensure bilingual elements are present. |
| 10 min | Celebration & Confidence Check | "On a scale of 1–5, how much have you grown from Week 1? What's your next step after the Dreamer AI Lab?" — Celebrate with a small acknowledgment (certificate, sticker, or verbal praise). |

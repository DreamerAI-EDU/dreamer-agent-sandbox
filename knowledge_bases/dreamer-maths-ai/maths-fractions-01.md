# Fractions in Game Design: Probability and Balance

## Dream Phase: Sparking Mathematical Curiosity Through Play

What do fractions and game design have in common? More than you might think. Every time a game designer decides how often a rare item drops, or how much damage a weapon deals, fractions and probability are at work behind the scenes.

### The Big Question

**If you were designing a boss battle, how would you use fractions to make it challenging but fair?**

### Fractions Are Everywhere in Games

| Game Element | Fraction at Work | Example |
|-------------|-----------------|---------|
| Drop rates | `1/100` chance of rare loot | 1 in 100 kills drops the legendary sword |
| Damage scaling | `3/4` of enemy HP with critical hit | A critical hit takes 75% of enemy health |
| Probability | `1/6` chance to roll a six on a die | Dice-based board game mechanics |
| Resource management | `2/3` of mana bar remaining | Should you cast a spell now or save it? |

### Activity: Design Your Own Loot Table

Students work in small groups to design a loot table for a fantasy game boss.

**Constraints:**
- The boss drops exactly 3 items when defeated
- Each drop slot has different probability rules
- All probabilities must be expressed as fractions that sum correctly

**Template:**

| Drop Slot | Possible Items | Drop Chance (as fraction) |
|-----------|---------------|---------------------------|
| Slot 1 (guaranteed) | Common currency | `1/1` |
| Slot 2 (equipment) | Sword or Shield | Sword `1/2`, Shield `1/2` |
| Slot 3 (rare) | Legendary Gem or Nothing | Gem `1/10`, Nothing `9/10` |

### Discussion: Why Fractions Matter

After designing their tables, groups present and answer:

1. How did fractions help you describe uncertainty?
2. If a player kills the boss 100 times, how many Legendary Gems should they expect? Why might the actual number differ?
3. What would happen if you changed the Legendary Gem drop from `1/10` to `1/5`?

### Bridge to AI

Modern games sometimes use AI to dynamically adjust drop rates based on player behavior. If an AI notices a player is about to quit, should it secretly increase their odds? This connects to the Discover phase — understanding how data-driven systems make decisions.
